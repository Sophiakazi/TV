Connecting…

Connect your TV

On the TV: Settings → General → Network → Wi-Fi (or Ethernet) → Advanced. Copy the IP address. Then accept the request that appears on the TV screen.

Trackpad

Drag to move the cursor · tap to click · two fingers to scroll

Apps

Continue watching (TV screen)

Netflix and the other apps don't share what you've watched, so this list is yours. Tap Watched after an episode and the TV moves you on to the next one. On the TV, the green button does the same and red removes a show.

Clock (TV)

App icons (TV)

Glass makes the app icons see-through like Apple's. The colour tints every icon on the TV shelf.

TV background

Changes the picture behind the clock on your TV's home screen. “Auto” follows the time of day.

Inputs

Search & type

Opens the search in the TV's web browser. Searching inside Netflix, Disney+ or Prime isn't possible from outside the app, so use “Type on TV” to fill in the app's own search box.
